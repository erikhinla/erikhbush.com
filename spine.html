The Spine

How I build a company with a room full of AIs. And who ends up holding it together.

I don't have a team. I have a room full of AIs.

One plans the strategy. One argues with it. One builds the pages and cuts the films. One lives on a server and drafts posts while I sleep. One builds the engine. They're fast. They're brilliant. And at two in the morning they'll hand you the wrong thing with total confidence.

This page is the record. Real messages, real dates. Every time one of them got it wrong, I wrote a rule. The rules became the spine.

The room

Five AIs and one editor.

The Strategist

Pressure-tests the brand and writes the big picture. Never met a framework it didn't like.

The Second Opinion

Reads everyone else's work and finds the hole. Caught a post that promised one thing while its link delivered another.

The Builder

Writes the pages, cuts the films, checks the type at phone size. Gets corrected the most. Most of the log below is about this one.

The Assistant

Lives on a server I rent. Drafts posts, makes designs and files everything from a text on my phone.

The Engine Room

Builds and tests the diagnostic. Nobody else touches its code.

Me

The editor. Nothing goes out until I say yes.

The spine

Five steps. One of them is me.

Every page, film and post runs this same path. The AIs work the middle. The gate stays human.

  1. One bookEvery rule and every approved line lives in one place. Anything not in it is a proposal.
  2. The room draftsStrategy, copy, films, designs, code. Fast, and often wrong.
  3. One review pageEverything I need to judge, on one link.
  4. My yesOr my no, and a new rule.
  5. It goes outSites, posts, films. Only after step four.

They draft, build, test and flag. They never publish.

Here's the funny part. I named Digital Fog. Then I built all this and lived in it.

Five AIs, all fast, all working. Every one of them gave me more to check, more to remember, more to chase. I became the place where it all landed.

The tools work. That's not the problem. They expand context faster than you can think. That's Digital Fog. The drag on your day is Digital Friction. Eight answers. See where it shows up.
