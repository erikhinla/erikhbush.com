erikhbush.com is Erik's site. It has pages, films, and a few tools you can use in the browser.

Google, YouTube, and Meta

Erik uses Google and YouTube APIs, and Meta APIs for Facebook and Instagram, only to publish and manage content on his own accounts. These APIs are not used to collect information about visitors to this website, and they are not a service for anyone else's accounts.

Erik Bush's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
