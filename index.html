Digital De-Fog Daily / The practice

A finish line for the unfinished.

DDD runs on a 20-minute boundary. One surface, a few decisions, one action, and a place to pick it up again.

2 minutes · Choose

Pick one visible surface. Files, phone, messages, tabs or notes.

6 minutes · Sort

One decision per item: keep, move, let go, decide later. The log records the decision. The change happens in the original app.

8 minutes · Act

Pick one visible action and name what finished looks like. If it won't fit, shrink it.

4 minutes · Leave a way back

Record where the work lives and what happens next. Tomorrow starts with a place to return to.

How the Scan fits

The Scan describes a starting point. DDD uses it to suggest a surface, which can be changed. The Scan's score isn't PROOF, a diagnosis, or a measure of minutes saved.

DDD records what gets entered. The timer records elapsed session time. Nothing inspects your files, verifies completed work, or claims a productivity gain.

Make the work hold together.

We start with the work people are already carrying. We trace where the context gets lost, give it a home, and check that the next person can act.

01 / FoldersA place for it.

We give the work a clear home so the next person can find what they need.

02 / MarkdownContext that stays.

We write down the purpose, decisions and instructions in plain text that people and tools can read.

03 / ScriptsRepeat the useful part.

We turn agreed, repeatable steps into scripts and check their outputs.

04 / ProtocolsKnow what happens next.

We make ownership, permissions, checks and exceptions explicit.
